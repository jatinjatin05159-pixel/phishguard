          How the check works

          Four layers, so one weak signal never decides alone.

          First

          It reads the words

          A trained text model spots pressure, fake fees and scam wording, including Hinglish.

          Second

          It inspects the links

          Short links are expanded, then the site's age, a known-bad list and a top-sites list are checked.

          Third

          It matches scam patterns

          Digital arrest calls, fake APK apps, OTP requests and fake agencies.

          Last

          It explains and guides

          You see the exact words behind each warning, then clear steps to stay safe.

          What we cannot do. We cannot see who really sent a message or their phone number, and we do not verify UPI IDs or bank accounts. No tool catches every scam. When unsure, call your bank on the number printed on your card.

          Four scams worth knowing

          These cause the most harm. Read them once and you will spot them next time.

          Digital arrest

          A caller says they are police, CBI or customs and that you are linked to a crime. They keep you on a video call and demand money to "clear your name".

          No agency arrests anyone on a video call or takes money to close a case. Hang up and call 1930.

          Fake apps and remote access

          A link asks you to install an .apk file, or someone asks you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer. The app then reads your OTPs and controls your phone.

          Install apps only from the Play Store, and never share your screen with a stranger.

          Fake KYC and bank alerts

          A message says your account will be blocked unless you update KYC or PAN. The link leads to a copy of your bank's page that steals your login.

          Banks do not ask for KYC through SMS links. Open the bank's own app instead.

          Fake courier and bill messages

          "Your parcel is held, pay Rs 49" or "power will be cut tonight". The small fee gives scammers your card details.

          Check parcels and bills in the official app, never through a link in a message.
